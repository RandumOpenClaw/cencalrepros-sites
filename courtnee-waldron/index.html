About Courtnee

Creative Mind,
Real Results

Courtnee Waldron is a REALTOR® with the Love JMA Team at CenCal Repros whose enthusiasm for real estate is matched only by her commitment to the people she serves. She approaches each transaction as a unique opportunity to connect the right person with the right property.

Known for her creative marketing, sharp eye for value, and genuine warmth with clients, Courtnee brings a distinctive energy to the team. Her clients don't just get a good agent — they get an advocate who genuinely cares about the outcome long after closing day.

A proud Central Valley local, Courtnee stays plugged into the community and ahead of market trends so her clients always have the best information to make confident decisions.
