About Courtnee

Kind Yet Direct.
Fierce Negotiator.

I'm Courtnee Waldron — your Central California Home Advisor, dedicated agent on the Love JMA Team, and someone who genuinely loves real estate and people in equal measure.

A Fresno/Clovis native through and through, I attended Clovis West High School and graduated from Fresno State in 1998. The Central Valley isn't just where I work. It's home. I proudly serve buyers and sellers across Fresno, Clovis, Madera, Hanford, and the greater Central Valley.

My job isn't just to help you buy or sell. It's to walk alongside you through one of the biggest financial decisions of your life with honesty, expertise, and genuine care.

My approach is simple.

Faith. Service. Love.

Your best interest is always my top priority.
