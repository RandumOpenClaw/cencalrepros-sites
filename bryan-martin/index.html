About Bryan

Experience You
Can Count On

Bryan Martin is a licensed California Real Estate Broker and co-owner of CenCal Repros. His career spans two decades of hands-on deal-making across Fresno County and the broader Central Valley — from first-time buyer transactions to complex multi-property negotiations.

As a broker, Bryan holds himself and the entire team to a higher standard. Clients benefit directly from his oversight, experience, and no-nonsense approach to getting deals across the finish line.

Whether you're buying, selling, or building an investment portfolio, Bryan brings a steady hand, sharp market intelligence, and a commitment to results that only comes from years of doing this work at the highest level.
