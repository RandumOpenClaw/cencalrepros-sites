About Sally

Steady Hand,
Smart Results

Sally O'Brien is a highly experienced REALTOR® with the Love JMA Team whose track record speaks for itself. With over 150 transactions closed and years of deep market immersion in the Central Valley, she brings a level of expertise that clients immediately recognize and trust.

Known for her calm, professional demeanor and clear communication, Sally navigates even the most complex transactions with confidence — keeping her clients informed, protected, and moving forward at every stage.

A proud member of CenCal Repros, Sally shares the team's commitment to doing things the right way: honest advice, skilled negotiation, and results that hold up long after closing day.
