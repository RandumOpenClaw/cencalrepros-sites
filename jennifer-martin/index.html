About Jennifer

Building Legacies,
One Home at a Time

Jennifer Martin is the Broker and Owner of CenCal Repros and founder of the Love JMA Team — one of Central California's most respected real estate organizations. With over two decades in the industry, she leads by example, still working directly with buyers and sellers every day while mentoring the next generation of agents.

Her philosophy is simple: every client deserves broker-level attention. That commitment to quality runs through everything the Love JMA Team does, from first showing to final signature.

A recognized leader in the Fresno County Association of REALTORS®, Jennifer brings a depth of market knowledge that only comes from years of doing the work at the highest level.
