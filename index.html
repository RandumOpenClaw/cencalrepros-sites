Who We Are

Built on Care,
Driven by Results

Love JMA Team is a close-knit group of seven REALTORS® based in California's Central Valley. We operate under CenCal Repros with a shared mission: give every client — whether they're buying their first home or selling the family house — expert, personalized service they'll actually want to talk about.

Our team was built with intention. Founded by Jennifer Martin, one of the Valley's most experienced brokers, Love JMA brings together agents at every stage of their career — seasoned veterans and rising talent, united by the same standards and the same genuine love for helping people.

We cover Fresno, Clovis, Madera, and the surrounding communities. Whatever your real estate goal, one of us is your person.
